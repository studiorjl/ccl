Highland Views Glenmore Park, a project delivered by CCL Development. Bordered by national parks and bushland corridors, the estate integrates walking tracks, an outdoor gym network, cycleways, local sporting ovals, and playground facilities at Pinnacle Park. The estate features around 300 to 350 residential lots ranging from under 350 m² to over 700 m².
